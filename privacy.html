Privacy policy

Effective November 14, 2026

This policy covers HQ, the private software Sports Receipts uses to manage its own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts, and this website. HQ has a single user: the owner of the Sports Receipts channels. It is not offered to the public.

Use of YouTube API Services

HQ uses the YouTube API Services. By using HQ you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What HQ accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, HQ can access, for the Sports Receipts channel only:

  • Channel and video statistics: views, likes, comments, watch time, average percentage viewed and subscribers gained or lost.
  • The list of the channel's own videos and their titles and publish dates.
  • Permission to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and privacy status, used only after the owner approves each video.

If TikTok, Instagram or Facebook (Meta) are connected, HQ accesses the equivalent information for the Sports Receipts accounts on those platforms only: basic profile or Page details, video and post statistics, and permission to post videos the owner has approved.

HQ does not access, collect or store information about viewers, commenters or any other users.

How the information is used

The information is used only to show the owner how the channel's videos perform and to post videos the owner has approved. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone.

Where it is stored

Everything HQ collects is stored on the owner's own computer, in a local database. Sign-in tokens are kept in a private folder on that computer that only the owner can read. Nothing is stored on a server or in the cloud by HQ. Statistics are refreshed at least every few hours and older copies are replaced. Stored statistics are deleted when the owner disconnects the account or deletes HQ.

Cookies and this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time:

  • Google: open the Google security settings page, choose HQ and remove access.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy, then Security, then Manage app permissions, and remove HQ.
  • Instagram: go to Settings, then Website permissions (Apps and websites), and remove HQ.
  • Facebook: go to Settings and privacy, then Settings, then Business integrations (Apps and websites), and remove HQ.

After access is removed, HQ can no longer read statistics or post, and the owner can delete the locally stored data.

Deleting your data

HQ only holds data about the Sports Receipts accounts, never about other people. To have any of that data deleted, remove HQ's access as described above, or email sportsreceiptsnow@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". The locally stored statistics and sign-in tokens are deleted within 30 days, and you will get a reply confirming it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
